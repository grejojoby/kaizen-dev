# Code of Conduct

Kaizen Dev is a community project. We want participation to be respectful, inclusive, and focused on improving the work.

Examples of welcomed behavior include listening to different experiences, giving and receiving constructive feedback, assuming good faith while addressing impact, and making room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disruption, or sharing someone else's private information without consent. Critique ideas and changes, not a contributor's identity or worth.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate this policy. If you experience or witness conduct that concerns you, contact the project maintainer privately through the repository's contact method. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.

This policy is intended to support a welcoming project. It does not limit good-faith disagreement about technical choices.
